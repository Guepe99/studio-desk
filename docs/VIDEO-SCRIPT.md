# Video script

A demo of about 115 seconds, under the two-minute limit. Judges may score an entry from the video and the text alone, and may stop watching at two minutes (Official Rules, sections 4.4, 4.8 and 6), so the video shows what each judging criterion asks for while it shows the product working. It follows one producer through one match, M001, in the order of the brief's pipeline: the problem, events turned into facts, the agents at work, an edit the desk refuses within the first 41 seconds, her decision on one story, the same goal for three audiences, a viewer's question, the enterprise controls, the measured numbers, a change of shape the agents read, what is missing today with the roadmap, and the address.

## Voice and sound

The voice-over is the Kokoro voice `am_michael` (fetched by `scripts/booth-kokoro-setup.sh`), in English. There is no music and the page's own **Voice** stays off. Silences longer than half a second are cut with `silencedetect`.

The timings below use a speaking rate of 2.20 words a second, measured on the 83 English story clips of the replays (1,362 words in 620.2 seconds, `replays/<match>/voice.json`). Each segment gets its speaking time plus a 0.5-second pause, rounded up to the half second. Time the rendered Kokoro track and move the cuts to it; there are 5.0 seconds of margin before the limit.

## Picture

- The site at 1920x1080, without the browser frame, tabs or address bar. All links are relative to `https://studiodesk.calmmoss-3f076252.swedencentral.azurecontainerapps.io/`; `t` is match time in seconds.
- 3D needs a graphics card. On the build server it runs at about 0.2 frames a second, so the 3D shots are rendered frame by frame with the existing 3D video tooling (about 35 minutes for 20 seconds at 30 fps) and the desk is recorded on its own, then both are put side by side in the edit.
- Edited with Remotion in `video/`: zooms on the part of the screen that matters, captions that carry the point with the sound off, and four cards of our own (the pipeline, the three audiences, the fallback test, the numbers) and two labels that stay honest about the footage: the replay is recorded agent output, and the 3D is rendered from the same tracking data. Captions are short, in English, without em dashes.
- Microsoft product names (the sponsor's hero technologies) are spoken and written in captions; no logos of any kind, and no third-party brand on screen. Show the site only, never the code host or the Azure portal.
- Any text on screen that is not English (the Italian goal card) gets an English subtitle under it (Official Rules, section 4.9).

The Foundry groundedness score is left out of the video: it scored the on-air texts, which were all the code's phrasebook lines.
