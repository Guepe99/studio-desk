# Video script

A demo of 115.5 seconds, under the two-minute limit. Section 6 of the Official Rules scores five criteria, so the video covers each of them while it shows the product working. It follows one producer through one match, M001, in the order of the brief's pipeline: the problem, events turned into facts, the agents at work, an edit the desk refuses within the first 37 seconds, her decision on one story, the goal in 3D with the site's own commentary, the same goal for four audiences at once, a viewer's question, the enterprise controls, the measured numbers, a change of shape the agents read, what is missing today with the roadmap, and the address.

## Voice and sound

The voice-over is eleven lines read by the Kokoro voice `am_michael` (fetched by `scripts/booth-kokoro-setup.sh`), in English, one WAV per line made by `video/voice.py` from `video/voice-jobs.json`. The goal has no narrator: it plays the booth's own goal call from the replay (`sfx/goal-call.wav`), in English, Italian and Spanish. There is no music. Under the voice runs the site's own CC0 crowd sound (`web/vendor/crowd-audio`, [THIRD_PARTY.md](../THIRD_PARTY.md)), low under the desk and louder under the 3D clips, with a whistle at the start, the crowd's anticipation from 0.3 s, an ooh at 4.9 s as the big chance goes wide and the roar at 54.0 s for the goal. The crowd fades out over the last 2.5 seconds.

The video opens on 0.3 seconds of plain background, and each segment lasts its voice line plus a short pause, so a segment starts 0.3 seconds plus the lengths of the segments before it (`SEGMENTS` in `video/src/Root.jsx`). The "speech (s)" column is the length of each WAV measured with `ffprobe`, silence included: each file starts with about 0.4 seconds of silence. Three files are longer than their segments (v01, v05, v10); they end with 0.57 to 0.71 seconds of silence, so only silence is cut. The cut is 115.5 seconds, 4.5 seconds under the limit.

## Picture

- The site at 1920x1080, without the browser frame, tabs or address bar. All links are relative to `https://studiodesk.calmmoss-3f076252.swedencentral.azurecontainerapps.io/`; `t` is match time in seconds.
- Everything on screen except the cards comes from the live site, in a Chromium that draws WebGL 2 in software (SwiftShader, started by `video/capture.sh`). The desk is shown as stills, one per shot, made by `video/capture.mjs`; each capture starts as a new visitor, so one capture's decisions do not show in the next. The 3D is three short clips made by `video/clips.mjs` from the same page: the page runs on Playwright's fake clock, each video frame advances it by 1/30 s before the picture is saved, so the software renderer's slowness never shows, and the capture stops if the replay clock is more than 0.5 s off. The multiview's large window is a still of the same 3D view (`view=3d`).
- Edited with Remotion in `video/`: slow zooms and pans across the stills, captions that carry the point with the sound off, three cards of our own (the pipeline, the fallback test, the numbers) and the end card, and two labels that stay honest about the footage. On every desk still, bottom right: "Live site, stills · replay of agent output recorded on Azure". On the 3D clips, top right over the stands: "Live site · 3D replay of the synthetic match, recorded frame by frame". The cards carry no label. Captions are short, in English, without em dashes.
- Microsoft product names (the sponsor's hero technologies) are spoken and written in captions; no logos of any kind, and no third-party brand on screen. Show the site only, never the code host or the Azure portal.
- Text on screen that is not English gets an English subtitle where it is large enough to read (Official Rules, section 4.9): when the Italian fan's window is large, its card is subtitled "Italian card: shot speed, Hugo Okowande, 92.1 km/h. Goal". In the four-window still the Italian and Spanish cards are small and carry the same numbers as the English cards beside them, and the Italian and Spanish goal call is captioned with its language only.

The voice-over is 240 words in eleven lines, 97.5 seconds of WAV including the silence at both ends of each file. Its numbers come from [docs/MEASURES.md](MEASURES.md) and the [README](../README.md): $0.062 to $0.103 a match in model calls (Content Safety not included); 668 Python and 282 browser-logic tests in CI; 11 of 14 planted storylines found by the agents and 9 by the rules alone; the offline run of 25 Oct 2026 in which every model call failed and M001 still put 15 stories on air in three languages (the card calls M001 "the test match"); managed identity for the model (Speech, used offline, and the Content Safety fallback still have keys, so the video says only the model); and the [Limits](../README.md#limits) and [Roadmap](../README.md#roadmap) for the last line. A refused edit is written to the audit log as `edit_refused` (`studiodesk/api.py`). The Foundry groundedness score is left out of the video: it scored the on-air texts, which were all the code's phrasebook lines.
